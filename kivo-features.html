STEP 01

Capture

Every enquiry lands in Kivo the moment it happens — no one has to remember to type it in. A WhatsApp keyword, a Meta lead-form submission, a walk-in note — all become one lead record automatically.

  • WhatsApp keyword triggers create a lead instantly
  • Meta / Facebook ad forms sync in real time
  • Manual entry for walk-ins and referrals, same pipeline

STEP 02

Assign

No more "whose lead is this" in the group chat. Kivo routes every lead using the rule you set — round robin, fair-share by workload, or a shared Collab Pool agents can pick up from — so the right person gets it in seconds.

  • Round-robin or fair-share assignment, your choice
  • Collab Pool lets any free agent grab an unclaimed lead
  • Fallback agent catches anything nobody's picked up

STEP 03

Engage

The first reply happens before your agent even opens the app. No-code reply flows catch common questions instantly, and broadcasts go out from your own WhatsApp number — so it still feels personal, not like a bot.

  • No-code reply flows triggered by keywords
  • Broadcasts sent from your team's real numbers
  • Full chat history stays attached to the lead record
STEP 04

Meet

Once a viewing's confirmed, it goes on a shared appointment board — so if the original agent is stuck elsewhere, any free teammate can pick it up and run the showing without the lead ever knowing there was a handover.

  • Shared appointment board, visible to the whole team
  • Any available closer can cover a viewing
  • Outcome logged straight back onto the lead

STEP 05

Measure

Stop guessing which ad is actually working. Kivo tracks follow-up speed per agent and conversion per source, so you know exactly which Meta campaign is worth the spend — and which agent needs coaching.

  • Follow-up speed tracked per agent, automatically
  • Conversion rate broken down by ad / source
  • Weekly and monthly views, exportable
STEP 06

Notify

A new lead, a confirmed viewing, a closed deal — everyone who needs to know finds out the moment it happens, plus a daily digest so nothing quietly slips through the cracks overnight.

  • Instant pings for new leads, viewings, and closings
  • Daily digest so nothing's missed overnight
  • Works wherever your team already is — no new app to check
