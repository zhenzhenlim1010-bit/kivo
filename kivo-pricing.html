FAQ

Common questions

Everything you'd want to know before starting a trial.

What exactly does Kivo do?
Kivo takes leads coming into your agency, routes each one to the right agent automatically, and tracks it through to a closed deal — with reporting your whole team can see.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
Start a Standard or Pro trial with no card required. You get full access for 7 days, and can choose a plan — or downgrade to Free — any time before it ends.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes — upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and yearly billing at any time from your account settings.
How many team members can I add?
Free supports 1 seat, Standard supports up to 15, and Pro is unlimited — so it scales with the size of your agency.
Is my data secure?
Every lead and pipeline record is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is scoped per team member so agents only see what's assigned to them.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, with no lock-in. Cancel from your account settings and you'll keep access through the end of your current billing period.
